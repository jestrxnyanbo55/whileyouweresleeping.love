Dear Paige Hilda May Ventresca

It’s been a while since I sent you a paragraph while you slept, and it’s been a while since I’ve written you a letter. I wanted to do both but with you in Alabama and me in Utah that seemed a little tricky so this felt like the next best thing. I made this little website to send you a message that can exist as a digital representation of my love for you.

We have been an active part of each-others lives for over a year now and there’s so much I have learned from you. About myself, about my interests, about the fact that I don’t actually hate anime as much as I thought I did (Death Note is one of my favorites and I would never have watched it without you.) But this isn’t about me, this is about you.

You have grown so much as a person, a person who is persistent and hardworking and funny and smart and embodies qualities that even in a passing moment, a stranger would pick up on. You are so much a different person than when I first met you, but then again so am I. You have grown closer to your family, and while you may not remember this, it was something you were terrified of when we first got together. You have gone from almost fired at your job to a full on stylist with a repertoire of clients who admire and look forward to seeing you. you have grown into someone I feel proud to call my best friend, my partner, my lover, and my everything.

I love our little inside jokes, I adore the sound of your laughter like dry soil adores rain. I know this time apart is difficult and I don’t want you to feel like we’re growing apart, because that’s not my goal. In the day I have been here so much has happened and I promise I will tell you everything but for right now I need you to let your shoulders down and exhale and know that everything is going to be okay.

I am so thankful for you and I look forward to seeing and holding you again my love. You are my rock, my world, my koala. I love you

Yours unless it’s too much fun, Noah Deitsch

P.S. My favorite part of you is the crescent-sliver in your left eye that looks gold when the light catches it right.
